Privacy policy

Shorts Studio · last updated 27 September 2026

Who we are

Shorts Studio is a private tool operated by Alexis Robert, Route de la Fonderie 2, 1700 Fribourg, Switzerland (alexis.rob23@gmail.com). It is used only by its operator, for the operator's own YouTube channels.

Google data accessed

With the channel owner's authorization, Shorts Studio requests read-only access to YouTube Analytics and YouTube Data for the operator's own channels: video list, views, watch time and audience retention. It never uploads, edits or deletes content through this access, and never accesses data of other users.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to measure the performance of the operator's own videos. It is stored locally on the operator's computer, is never sold, shared or transferred to third parties, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models. The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Access tokens are kept on the operator's computer and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Analytics records are kept as long as the channels are active and deleted on request.

Cookies

These pages use no cookies and no tracking.
