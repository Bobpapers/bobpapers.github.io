Shorts Studio

Private publishing tool, operated by Alexis Robert, Fribourg, Switzerland.

Shorts Studio is the in-house tool used to prepare, schedule and follow the performance of short videos on five YouTube channels owned by its operator:

  • The Builder Minute (@thebuilderminute)
  • Deep Field Minute (@DeepFieldMinute)
  • Wow Lab (@WowLab-x4q)
  • Behind The Frame (@BehindTheFrame-z9l)
  • The Skyline Minute (@TheSkylineMinute)

It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts. It connects to Google only to read the YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, audience retention) of these channels, with read-only access.
